# Fridge text candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters, in the voice of an
appliance awake all night. Scored on timing, voice, punchline.

1. Conscious leftovers: "It's 3am and the leftovers from March
   have achieved consciousness. They are asking about you." --
   dread plus tenderness; the fridge as messenger of the thing
   it failed to preserve.
2. Door count: "3AM update: you opened me six times tonight. The
   answer is still no, and the pizza has filed a restraining
   order." -- funny, two jokes competing.
3. Milk report: "The milk expires tomorrow. It asked me to tell
   you it has no regrets, unlike someone at this address." --
   wistful, softer punch.
4. Temperature feelings: "I keep this house at 3 degrees for
   you. You keep opening me to stare like the food will change.
   It changes. Slowly. Like you." -- poetic, long.
5. Ice maker: "I made ice all night for a party you cancelled in
   a dream. We need to talk about boundaries." -- charming,
   fridge less central.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The draft's flat
"they are asking about you" became a small civilization: the
leftovers "elected a leader, and are asking when you'll be
home" -- the fridge now reports an organized society forming in
the dark, and the domestic question ("when you'll be home") is
what makes it a text message rather than a horror line. 118
characters, inside the 140 limit.
